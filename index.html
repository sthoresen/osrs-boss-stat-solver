How it works

The DPS maths

Every number uses the formulas of the OSRS Wiki DPS calculator (source), ported to a small engine fast enough to test thousands of bosses per second. It was checked against the real calculator on 300 random bosses × 13 setups, including drained bosses, and the results matched exactly.

Player: 99 in all combat stats with a super combat, a ranging potion and a saturated heart, plus Piety, Rigour or Augury. Item stats and icons come from the same calculator.

Item effects that are modelled include the Twisted bow's magic-level scaling (capped at 250), the Scythe's three hits (the boss is always assumed to be large), the Inquisitor set bonus, crystal armour with the Bowfa, ruby bolt procs, Twinflame's second hit and smoke-staff bonus, Shadow's ×3 gear bonus, Confliction gauntlets, tomes, and elemental weakness.

Defence drain

Each setup gets two values: one at full defence, and one at the lowest defence the boss allows. Defence drain cap is how much of its Defence level the boss can lose, like the defence floors on Nex (250) or Yama (145). DWH, elder maul, BGS and Ralos specs all stop at this floor. Drain helps melee and ranged only, because a boss's magic defence uses its Magic level.

The magic equivalent is the Eye of Ayak special attack, which drains the boss's magic defence bonus. Magic def drain cap limits how much of that bonus it can remove.

Elemental weakness has no type here: the weakness % applies to the elemental setups (Harmonised staff and Twinflame), which cast fire spells with a Tome of Fire. Since the elemental rework all surges hit 24 and all waves hit 20 at this Magic level, so the element makes no difference to max hit.

The solver

When you press Solve, each setup you nudged aims for its current DPS ±5% per click, and every other setup aims to stay where it is. The solver minimises the squared log-error against those aims, so being 10% off counts the same for a 5 DPS setup as for a 10 DPS one. It uses differential evolution and then a fine integer search, with a weak pull toward the current stats.

Some requests can't all be met at once. Bludgeon, Inq mace and Scythe crush all attack crush defence, so the boss can't favour one over another, and only their gear separates them. Magic level also does two jobs: it sets the magic defence roll and it scales the Twisted bow.

Not modelled: special attack damage, prayer drain, thralls, ruby bolts weakening as the boss loses HP, and boss attributes (demon, dragon, undead).
